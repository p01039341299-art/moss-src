Chicago Italian Beef Sandwich

Thin-sliced seasoned beef piled on a roll, dipped in jus, and topped with hot giardiniera.

By Elena Vargas · Tested twice in our kitchen

  • Cook210 min

About this recipe

Italian beef is Chicago’s sandwich — juicy, spicy, and meant to be eaten over a counter.

Slice the beef as thin as you can against the grain after a long rest.

Why this recipe works

  • A low braise makes the roast shreddy and tender.
  • Giardiniera cuts the richness.
  • Dipping the roll in jus is non-negotiable.
