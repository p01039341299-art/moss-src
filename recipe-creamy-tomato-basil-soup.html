Creamy Tomato Basil Soup

Silky roasted tomato soup finished with cream and plenty of fresh basil.

By Elena Vargas · Tested twice in our kitchen

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook35 min
  • Total50 min
  • Serves6
  • Calories220

About this recipe

A bowl of tomato soup next to a grilled cheese is Chicago rainy-day food. Roasting the tomatoes concentrates the sweetness.

Blend until completely smooth, then finish with cream off the heat.

Why this recipe works

  • Roasting deepens tomato flavor.
  • Basil keeps it bright.
  • A splash of cream rounds the acidity.

What you’ll need

  • 2 1/2 lb tomatoes, halved
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • + 5 more ingredients and 5 numbered steps

Variations

  • Add roasted red peppers.
  • Use coconut milk.
  • Top with grilled cheese croutons.

How to serve

With a grilled cheese sandwich.

Storage and reheating

Cool leftovers within 2 hours and refrigerate in a covered container for up to 4 days. Reheat gently on the stove or in a 325°F oven with a splash of water or broth so nothing dries out. Stews, braises, and baked pasta also freeze well for up to 3 months.

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss tomatoes, onion, and garlic with oil, salt, and pepper. Roast 25 minutes.
  2. Transfer to a pot with broth and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Stir in cream and torn basil. Adjust salt and sugar.
  5. Serve hot.

Tips for success

  • Use a mix of ripe tomatoes for best flavor.
  • Strain for an ultra-smooth texture.
  • Freeze without cream and add it later.
