Homemade Chicago Giardiniera

Crunchy pickled vegetables in a spicy oil-vinegar brine — the condiment Chicago runs on.

By Elena Vargas · Tested twice in our kitchen

  • Prep30 min
  • Cook0 min
  • Total30 min
  • Serves16
  • Calories90

About this recipe

Giardiniera belongs on Italian beef, pizza, eggs, and anything that needs a kick.

Salt the vegetables overnight so they stay crisp in the jar.

Why this recipe works

  • Overnight salting keeps crunch.
  • Hot peppers define Chicago style.
  • Oil and vinegar balance heat and tang.

What you’ll need

  • 2 cups cauliflower florets
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery ribs, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 cup sliced serrano or jalapeño peppers
  • + 6 more ingredients and 5 numbered steps

Variations

  • Make it mild with only sweet peppers.
  • Add olives.
  • Chop fine for a relish.

How to serve

On sandwiches, pizza, and eggs.

Storage and reheating

Mashed potatoes keep 4 days in the fridge and reheat well in a 325°F oven with a splash of milk. Coleslaw keeps 2 days and must stay cold. Cornbread is best the day it is baked; wrap leftovers and warm them in the oven. Roasted Brussels reheat well in a hot skillet.

Instructions

  1. Toss vegetables with salt, cover with water, and refrigerate overnight.
  2. Drain and rinse well.
  3. Pack into jars with garlic, oregano, and peppercorns.
  4. Heat vinegar just to a simmer, cool slightly, and pour over vegetables. Top with olive oil.
  5. Refrigerate at least 2 days before using.

Tips for success

  • Use gloves with hot peppers.
  • Keep jars submerged under oil.
  • Lasts 3 to 4 weeks in the fridge.
