Mushroom Spinach Frittata

Oven-finished eggs with sautéed mushrooms, wilted spinach, and melted Gruyère.

By Elena Vargas · Tested twice in our kitchen

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Total35 min
  • Serves6
  • Calories280

About this recipe

A frittata is the fastest Chicago brunch when guests show up hungry. One skillet, no flipping stress.

Cook the vegetables until their moisture is gone so the eggs set cleanly.

Why this recipe works

  • Sautéed mushrooms add savory depth.
  • Spinach wilts into the eggs without watering them down.
  • Gruyère melts into nutty pockets.

What you’ll need

  • 8 eggs
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • + 5 more ingredients and 5 numbered steps

Variations

  • Add roasted red peppers.
  • Use feta instead of Gruyère.
  • Fold in leftover roasted potatoes.

How to serve

With toast and a green salad.

Storage and reheating

Most breakfast dishes keep 3 days in the fridge. Bagels are best assembled fresh. Baked oatmeal and cinnamon rolls freeze for up to 2 months — reheat rolls for 15 seconds in the microwave. Cook eggs fresh each time.

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Whisk eggs, milk, salt, and pepper.
  2. Sauté mushrooms and shallot in oil in an oven-safe skillet 6 to 8 minutes. Add spinach until wilted.
  3. Pour in eggs, sprinkle cheese, and cook on the stove 2 minutes until edges set.
  4. Bake 12 to 15 minutes until just set.
  5. Rest 5 minutes, scatter chives, and slice.

Tips for success

  • Pull when the center still jiggles slightly.
  • Use a well-seasoned cast-iron skillet.
  • Serve warm or at room temperature.
