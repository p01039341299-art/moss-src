About this recipe

Cornbread meets Chicago cheddar in a hot skillet. The edges fry in butter as it bakes.

Preheat the skillet so you hear a sizzle when the batter hits.

Why this recipe works

  • A hot skillet makes a crisp crust.
  • Cheddar melts into savory pockets.
  • Chives keep it fresh.

Storage and reheating
