Crispy Oven Potato Wedges

Thick-cut russet wedges roasted until shatter-crisp with paprika and garlic.

By Elena Vargas · Tested twice in our kitchen

  • Prep15 min
  • Cook40 min
  • Total55 min
  • Serves6
  • Calories260

About this recipe

These wedges beat fries when you want less oil and the same crunch.

Soak, dry, and give them space on the pan.

Why this recipe works

  • Soaking removes surface starch.
  • High heat crisps the edges.
  • Paprika and garlic season every side.

What you’ll need

  • 3 lb russet potatoes
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • + 2 more ingredients and 5 numbered steps

Variations

  • Add Parmesan in the last 5 minutes.
  • Serve with ranch.
  • Use sweet potatoes.

How to serve

With burgers or as a snack with ketchup.

Storage and reheating

Mashed potatoes keep 4 days in the fridge and reheat well in a 325°F oven with a splash of milk. Coleslaw keeps 2 days and must stay cold. Cornbread is best the day it is baked; wrap leftovers and warm them in the oven. Roasted Brussels reheat well in a hot skillet.

Yes. Coleslaw can be made a day ahead. Mashed potatoes hold warm in a covered pot. Bake cornbread just before serving.

Instructions

  1. Cut potatoes into wedges. Soak in cold water 20 minutes, then dry thoroughly.
  2. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss with oil and spices.
  3. Spread on two sheet pans without crowding.
  4. Roast 35 to 40 minutes, flipping once, until deep golden.
  5. Finish with parsley and more salt.

Tips for success

  • Do not skip drying.
  • Use two pans if needed.
  • Serve immediately.
