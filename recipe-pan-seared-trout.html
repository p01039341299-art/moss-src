Pan-Seared Trout with Lemon

Crisp-skinned trout fillets seared in butter and finished with lemon and parsley.

By Elena Vargas · Tested twice in our kitchen

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook12 min
  • Total22 min
  • Serves4
  • Calories320

About this recipe

Fresh trout cooks in minutes and tastes clean and lake-bright.

Press the fillets gently so the skin stays in contact with the pan.

Why this recipe works

  • High heat crisps the skin.
  • Butter basting keeps the flesh moist.
  • Lemon finishes without a heavy sauce.

What you’ll need

  • 4 trout fillets
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • + 2 more ingredients and 5 numbered steps

Variations

  • Add capers to the butter.
  • Use salmon.
  • Serve over lentils.

How to serve

With roasted potatoes and greens.

Storage and reheating

Seafood leftovers keep 2 days in the fridge. Reheat chowder gently on the stove without boiling. Crab cakes and fried fish are best recrisped in a 375°F oven. Do not leave cooked seafood at room temperature more than 2 hours.

Instructions

  1. Pat fish dry and season.
  2. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high. Place fillets skin-side down and press lightly 30 seconds.
  3. Cook 3 to 4 minutes until skin is crisp. Flip and cook 1 to 2 minutes.
  4. Add butter and baste.
  5. Serve with lemon and parsley.

Tips for success

  • Dry skin is essential for crispness.
  • Do not move the fish until it releases.
  • Pull at 145°F.
